Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions about REACT's air ambulance services in Bangalore.

How fast can REACT reach Bangalore?

REACT maintains an 18-minute response time in Bangalore. Our crews and aircraft are on standby 24/7 at Kempegowda International Airport, ensuring rapid mobilisation across Bengaluru and the surrounding Karnataka region.

Which airports does REACT use in Bangalore?

REACT primarily operates through Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) for fixed-wing missions. Helicopter operations and intra-city transfers can be coordinated via HAL Airport and hospital-attached helipads.

Which hospitals does REACT serve in Bangalore?

REACT coordinates with Manipal Hospital, Apollo Hospitals, Narayana Hrudayalaya, Fortis, and St. John's Medical College Hospital, among other leading facilities across north, south, and east Bengaluru.

Can REACT transport patients from Bangalore to smaller Karnataka cities?

Yes. REACT operates across Karnataka, connecting Mysuru, Hubli, Mangaluru, Belagavi, and other cities to Bengaluru's advanced hospital network via air ambulance — far faster than road transport for critical patients.
