Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions about REACT's air ambulance services in Chennai.

How fast can REACT reach Chennai?

REACT targets an 18-minute response time in Chennai. Our on-call medical crews and standby aircraft at Chennai International Airport allow rapid mobilisation for emergencies across the city and surrounding Tamil Nadu region.

Which airports does REACT use in Chennai?

REACT operates from Chennai International Airport (MAA) in Meenambakkam for all fixed-wing and turboprop missions, supporting both domestic and international departures to cover all transfer types.

Which hospitals does REACT serve in Chennai?

REACT coordinates with Apollo Hospitals, Fortis Malar Hospital, MIOT International, Sri Ramachandra Medical Centre, and Kauvery Hospital, among other leading facilities across Chennai's hospital belt.

Does REACT handle medical tourism repatriation through Chennai?

Yes. Chennai is a premier medical tourism hub. REACT facilitates international repatriation for patients from Sri Lanka, the Maldives, and Southeast Asia who have received treatment in Chennai and need a medically escorted flight home.
