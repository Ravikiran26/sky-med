Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about air ambulance services in Hyderabad.

How fast can REACT reach Hyderabad?

REACT maintains an 18-minute response time in Hyderabad. Our crews and aircraft are on standby 24/7, ensuring rapid mobilisation for any medical emergency in the city or surrounding Telangana region.

Which airports does REACT use in Hyderabad?

REACT operates primarily through Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) in Shamshabad. We also coordinate with private helipads at KIMS and AIG hospitals for direct rooftop landings when available and appropriate.

Which hospitals does REACT serve in Hyderabad?

REACT has established coordination with KIMS (Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences), Apollo Hospitals Jubilee Hills, AIG Hospitals Gachibowli, Yashoda Hospital, and other tertiary care centres across the city.

Does REACT handle neonatal and ECMO transport in Hyderabad?

Yes. REACT operates dedicated neonatal transport incubators and ECMO retrieval capability from Hyderabad. Our specialist crews include neonatologists and perfusionists for the most critical paediatric and adult cases.
