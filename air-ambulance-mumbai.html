Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about REACT's air ambulance services in Mumbai.

How fast can REACT reach Mumbai?

REACT targets an 18-minute response time in Mumbai. We maintain 24/7 standby crews at both CSMIA and Juhu Aerodrome, covering all corners of the city rapidly while accounting for Mumbai's traffic by prioritising air response.

Which airports does REACT use in Mumbai?

REACT operates from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) for fixed-wing aircraft and Juhu Aerodrome for helicopter operations. This dual-facility capability gives us maximum flexibility across the MMR.

Which hospitals does REACT serve in Mumbai?

REACT coordinates with Lilavati Hospital, Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, P.D. Hinduja Hospital, Tata Memorial Centre, and many other hospitals across South Mumbai, the suburbs, and Navi Mumbai.

Can REACT transport patients from Mumbai to international destinations?

Yes. CSMIA is a major international hub. REACT facilitates medically escorted international repatriation from Mumbai, handling aviation permits, medical documentation, and destination-side coordination for a seamless transfer.
