Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know before making the call.

How quickly can NIAMS deploy an aircraft?
Our average deployment time is 18 minutes from call confirmation to aircraft wheels-up. Our 24/7 command centre triages every call immediately and scrambles the nearest available aircraft with the right medical crew for your patient's condition.
What medical conditions can be transported?
We transport all critical care conditions including cardiac emergencies, stroke, multi-trauma, respiratory failure, neonatal and pediatric critical illness, post-surgical transfers, and patients requiring ECMO support. Our medical directors assess each case individually.
Does health insurance cover air ambulance services?
Many health insurance policies in India now cover air ambulance transport. We assist with all insurance documentation and work directly with TPA desks. We recommend calling us immediately — we handle the paperwork while the aircraft is en route.
What cities and regions does NIAMS cover?
We cover 32+ cities across India including Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, and all major Tier-2 cities. International operations are available on request for repatriation to and from any country.
What medical staff are on board?
Every NIAMS mission is staffed with a minimum of one flight physician and one flight nurse or paramedic. For specialist transfers (neonatal, ECMO, organ retrieval), we deploy condition-specific teams including neonatologists, perfusionists, and retrieval surgeons.
How do I request an air ambulance?
Call our 24/7 emergency line: 1800-NIAMS-911. Have ready: patient name, current location, diagnosis/condition, receiving hospital if known. Our team handles everything else — aircraft, crew, ground ambulances, documentation, and hospital coordination.
What equipment is on board every aircraft?
All NIAMS aircraft carry: ICU-grade ventilator, 12-lead cardiac monitor and defibrillator, IV infusion pumps, portable oxygen, suction unit, full drug kit, and trauma supplies. Specialist equipment (neonatal incubator, ECMO circuit, portable ultrasound) is added based on the clinical requirement.
Can NIAMS assist with non-emergency medical travel?
Yes. Through our Concierge Medical Travel service, we assist with hospital selection, specialist appointments, premium medical transport, accommodation, visa coordination, and post-treatment repatriation — a complete end-to-end service for planned medical journeys.
