Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  NL Curantis International Pvt Ltd

REACT Air Ambulance (operated by NL Curantis International Pvt Ltd) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all patient and user information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is safeguarded.

01

Information We Collect

When you contact us or use our services, we may collect:

  • Personal identification: Name, age, gender, nationality
  • Medical information: Diagnosis, current condition, treatment history, and other clinical details required to plan and execute safe medical transport
  • Contact details: Phone number, email address, and location
  • Payment information: Billing details processed securely through authorised payment gateways
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited on our website (via cookies and analytics tools)
02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • Planning and coordinating your air ambulance or medical transport mission
  • Communicating with you regarding your booking, flight status, and medical team
  • Coordinating with receiving hospitals, referring physicians, and ground transport teams
  • Processing payments and maintaining financial records as required by law
  • Improving our website, services, and operational protocols
  • Complying with regulatory and legal obligations (DGCA, health authorities)
03

Medical Data Confidentiality

All patient medical data is treated as strictly confidential. We follow medical ethics guidelines and applicable Indian health data regulations. Patient information is:

  • Shared only with clinical staff directly involved in the patient's care
  • Never sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for marketing purposes
  • Retained only for the period required by law and clinical standards
04

Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Medical partners: Receiving hospitals, referring doctors, and specialist consultants involved in your care
  • Aviation authorities: DGCA and other regulatory bodies as legally required for flight operations
  • Service providers: Payment processors and IT service providers operating under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal authorities: Where disclosure is required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of individuals
05

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website uses cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous. You may disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to contact us.

06

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. All sensitive data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data where legally permissible
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nlcurantisinternational@gmail.com.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date of last revision. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
